Privacy Policy

Sapiora — Read. Study. Master languages.

Effective date: October 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Sapiora Android application ("Sapiora", "we", "our") handles information when you use the app. Sapiora can be used without creating or signing in to an account. The app is designed to keep applicable study and reading data on your device, while some features use online services when you actively use them.

1. Accounts and information you provide

Sapiora does not require an account in the current version. The app does not require you to provide your name, phone number, or email address to use its core features. We do not sell users' personal information.

2. Data stored on your device

Where applicable, Sapiora stores app and study data locally on your device. This can include:

  • Library information and references to PDFs available on your device.
  • Highlights, underlines, notes, bookmarks and reading progress.
  • Saved vocabulary and other locally stored study data.
  • AI Assistant conversation history that is stored locally by the app.

This local data is not uploaded merely because you use the reader or study features. PDFs are accessed locally for reading and are not uploaded merely for opening or reading them.

3. Online features and information sent from the device

Sapiora uses network access only when an online feature requires it or when you actively use that feature. Depending on the feature, information may be sent to the relevant service:

  • Translation. When you actively request a translation and an offline translation is unavailable, selected text may be sent to Google Translate to obtain the requested translation.
  • AI Assistant. When you actively use the AI Assistant, your request and relevant content are sent to the AI backend/service configured by the developer for processing and generation of a response.
  • AI images. If you choose to attach or capture an image/photo for the AI Assistant, that image is transmitted to the configured AI service for processing together with the AI request.
  • Current affairs and other online content. Features that provide online current-affairs/news or other online content may connect to the online services used by Sapiora to retrieve that content.

AI conversation history may remain stored locally on your device. Data handling by an external service is also subject to that service's own privacy policy and terms.

4. Advertising and Google AdMob

Sapiora currently uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Because advertising is enabled, Sapiora should not be considered an ad-free or tracking-free app.

When ads are requested or displayed, Google/AdMob may process information such as device information or identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location, and information about ad delivery or interaction, as applicable to Google's advertising services and the device/platform settings.

Depending on Sapiora's AdMob configuration, applicable consent choices, and available settings, users may receive personalized or non-personalized advertisements. Google's processing of advertising data is governed by Google's applicable policies and settings.

5. Permissions and device access

  • All-files access — used to discover and list PDF files already available on your device so you can open them in the reader. Reading a PDF does not by itself upload the file.
  • Camera — used only when you choose to capture or attach an image for the AI Assistant.
  • Internet/network access — used for features that require online services, such as the AI Assistant, online translation when needed, current-affairs/news retrieval, and advertising.

6. Children's privacy

Sapiora is intended for students and general users rather than specifically for children under 13. The current version does not require an account or ask users to provide age or other personal information to use the app. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Parents or guardians who believe a child has provided personal information can contact us using the address below.

7. Data security and retention

Local app data remains on the user's device unless the user actively uses a feature that transmits information to an online service as described in this policy. We do not maintain a user account or a cloud account for Sapiora's local study history in the current version.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when Sapiora's features, services, or data practices change. The effective date above indicates when this version of the policy was last updated.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this policy or Sapiora's privacy practices, contact us at sapiora.app@gmail.com.
