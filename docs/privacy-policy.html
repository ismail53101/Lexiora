Privacy Policy

Sapiora — Read. Study. Master languages.

Effective date: August 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Sapiora Android application ("Sapiora", "we", "our") handles information when you use the app. Sapiora is designed to be offline-first: your library, highlights, notes, bookmarks, reading progress and saved vocabulary live on your device and never leave it unless you actively use a networked feature described below.

1. No account, no ads, no tracking

  • Sapiora does not require an account and does not collect your name, email or phone number.
  • Sapiora does not show ads.
  • Sapiora does not use analytics, advertising identifiers or third-party trackers.

2. Data stored on your device

The following stays in Sapiora's private on-device storage and is never uploaded on its own:

  • The PDF documents you read and the folders you grant access to (referenced in place, never copied).
  • Highlights, underlines, notes, bookmarks and reading progress.
  • Saved vocabulary and the offline dictionary/translation data bundled with the app.
  • Your AI Assistant conversations and settings.

3. When information leaves your device

Sapiora only sends data to the internet when you explicitly use a networked feature:

  • Translation. If a word has no offline translation, the selected word or phrase is sent to Google Translate (a free, keyless endpoint) to fetch a translation, which is then saved on your device.
  • AI Assistant. When you send a message — including any photo you attach — your prompt is sent to an artificial-intelligence service configured by the developer to generate a reply. Conversations are stored locally on your device.

We do not sell, rent or share your personal information with anyone.

4. Permissions we use

  • All-files access — used only to list the PDF files already on your device so you can open them in the reader. Files are referenced in place and are never modified or uploaded.
  • Camera — used only when you choose to attach a photo to the AI Assistant.
  • Internet — used only for the Translation and AI Assistant features described above.

5. Children's privacy

Sapiora is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Effective date" above reflects the latest revision.

7. Contact

If you have questions about this policy or the app, contact us at support@sapiora.app.
